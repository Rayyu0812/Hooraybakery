Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

01

Do you have a storefront?

We are a licensed home-based bakery — no storefront, but every product includes full ingredient details and our registration number. We bake in small batches with quality you can trust, right from our cozy kitchen in Irvine.

02

When are products available?

We bake to order, with pickup every Friday at 5 PM in Irvine, CA 92618. Free delivery within Irvine is available for orders of $30 or more on Fridays, with delivery timing confirmed by text. Please place your order at least 48 hours in advance.

03

Can I order outside fixed pickup times?

Absolutely! For custom pickup times, select the Custom option on the order page and fill in your Special Requests, or text us directly. We will reply as soon as possible and do our best to accommodate — timing may vary for special requests.
